Methodology

Source: five "Order History Ledger Report" exports from UK Textiles for McKenzie order codes MCKTM17373 (82/26 & its Poland split 130/26), MCKTM17405 (88/26), MCKTM17410 (89/26) and MCKTM17406 (90/26), printed 14 Aug 2026. Every department percentage is the ledger's own delivered/received department-total line — nothing here is re-estimated. Cutting and downstream figures use the PCS rollups printed at Cutting, Power Table, Fusing, Checking, Ironing and Packing. Department Health gauges are kg-weighted across every order that uses that department (received ÷ delivered, summed). No week-over-week trend data exists in the source ledgers — every badge on this page is a same-day comparison against order quantity or due date, not a historical trend.

Linked orders

82/26 and 130/26 are the same base PO (MCKTM17373) split by destination. 88/26, 89/26 and 90/26 are a linked McKenzie fleece hood + pant set — 89/26 and 90/26 draw on the same yarn lot, which is why their Yarn-department figures match exactly.
