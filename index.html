Delivery vs. Receipt — Balance reconciliation

Each department is reconciled on its own ledger lines. Fabric stages can include pooled lots, trim streams, reprocess, or transfers; garment stages are piece-based.
How to read stage increases
Logic check

A later department can show a larger input without duplicated order quantity. Yarn through Compacting are kg ledgers; Cutting through Packing are piece ledgers. Knitting can draw from pooled yarn shared by linked orders, while Compacting can receive rib and trim fabric directly without Brushing. Transfers, reprocess passes, opening stock, and new receipts also change a department's own balance. The dashboard therefore calculates department delivery − department receipt; it does not add stage totals together or pretend every row is one serial chain.

For example, 82/26 includes a 130 kg opening-stock receipt, 88/26 Compacting includes direct trim flow, and 90/26 shows 6,000 pieces at Assembly while the embroidery section is specifically for panel-front embroidery. The best-supported explanation is that other panels can reach Power Table without requiring embroidery; the exact panel split is still not itemized in the source.

Methodology

Twenty-five source PDFs were analyzed, all printed 21 Aug 2026: five Order History Ledger reports, five Diawise Program Balance reports, five Accessory Stock Itemwise reports, and ten Production Register — Running Orders reports. The current dashboard figures are driven by the Order History Ledger and Diawise balance totals; the Production Register is retained as a separate operational source until each department's party sum is checked against its printed total. Cumulative versus incremental treatment is therefore not assumed. Department delivery/receipt totals drive fabric health, while piece-based ledger totals drive Cutting through Packing. No week-over-week trend data exists in the reports.

Linked orders

82/26 and 130/26 are the same base PO (MCKTM17373) split by destination. 88/26, 89/26 and 90/26 are a linked McKenzie fleece hood + pant set — 89/26 and 90/26 draw on the same yarn lot, which is why their Yarn-department figures match exactly.
