Methodology

Three source systems, all printed 14 Aug 2026: (1) "Order History Ledger Report" — department-total delivered/received lines drive the KPI strip, bottleneck matrix and reconciliation table; (2) "Production Register — Running Orders" — a day-by-day, size-wise log used to independently re-derive the Cutting→Packing figures (every stage on 82/26, 88/26, 89/26 and 130/26 matched the ledger exactly, cross-checked in full); (3) "Accessory Stock Itemwise Report" — live PO/received/balance per component, backing the Accessories & Components section below. Nothing here is re-estimated — every figure traces to a printed total in one of these three reports. No week-over-week trend data exists in any of them — every badge on this page is a same-day comparison against order quantity, due date, or component balance, not a historical trend.

Linked orders

82/26 and 130/26 are the same base PO (MCKTM17373) split by destination. 88/26, 89/26 and 90/26 are a linked McKenzie fleece hood + pant set — 89/26 and 90/26 draw on the same yarn lot, which is why their Yarn-department figures match exactly.
